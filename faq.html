Questions before you get started?

Here are a few things worth knowing before starting a website project.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at the displayed prices for simple static websites. Final pricing depends on pages, features, content and any external services required.

Can I see examples first?

Yes. Visit the Portfolio page to see sample concepts. These are illustrative demos, not claimed client projects.

Will the website work on mobile?

The design is planned to adapt to common mobile, tablet and desktop screen sizes. Final testing depends on the project scope.

Is a domain and hosting included?

Not automatically. A free static hosting option may be suitable for a simple site, while custom domains or paid hosting can cost extra.

Can you build login, payments or a database?

Those features need backend setup and extra planning. They are not included in the basic static packages and must be quoted separately.

How do I start?

Use the Contact page to send your idea, website type and budget through WhatsApp. Agree on scope, price and delivery expectations before work begins.
